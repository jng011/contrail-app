2. About Contrail

Contrail is a golf shot tracer for iPhone. You place your iPhone on a stand or on the ground behind you, record a short range session, and the App finds each swing, cuts each one into its own clip and draws the ball's flight over the video. Shots are kept in a library on your device.

Contrail processes everything on your device. It has no account and no server, and we do not receive your recordings or any other data from the App. This shapes several of these Terms: we can't recover your recordings if you lose them, and we have no way to monitor or moderate what you film.

Contrail is currently in pre-release and is coming soon to the App Store. Features, availability and requirements may change before and after release (see Sections 11 and 30).

9. Golf and safety

Golf involves swinging a club at speed and hitting a hard ball that can travel fast and unpredictably. Using Contrail does not make golf any safer, and setting up a phone near where you swing creates its own risks. You agree to use the App responsibly and at your own risk. In particular:

  • Keep people clear. Before every swing, make sure no one is within reach of your club or in the path of the ball, including behind you and to the sides. Never let anyone stand near the phone while you are swinging.
  • Place the phone safely. Put your phone and stand where your club, your follow-through and the ball cannot hit them, and where nobody will trip over them. A ball can rebound off mats, dividers, posts and the ground.
  • Secure the stand. Use a stable stand or tripod rated for your phone, on level ground, and secure it against wind and knocks. A phone or stand that falls can break, and can injure someone.
  • Follow the facility's rules. Obey range and course rules, staff instructions and posted safety notices, including any rules about phones, tripods and recording. Stay in your bay or on your tee.
  • Pay attention. Don't look at or handle your phone while anyone nearby is swinging, or while balls are in the air. Watch for range vehicles.
  • Look after yourself. Warm up, stay hydrated, mind the heat, and stop if you feel pain. Talk to a doctor before taking up or changing a sport if you have health concerns.
  • Weather. Stop and seek shelter in lightning or severe weather.

10. No professional advice; tracer accuracy

The ball flight Contrail draws is a visual estimate, generated automatically from your recording. It is meant to help you see and enjoy your shots. It may be inaccurate or incomplete: it can miss shots, find shots that didn't happen, place the ball wrongly, or draw a flight that differs from what the ball actually did. Results depend on lighting, weather, background, framing, how steady the phone is, the device model and many other factors.

Contrail is not a launch monitor, rangefinder or measuring device, and it does not provide golf instruction or coaching, medical, fitness or physiotherapy advice, or any other professional advice. Don't rely on it for club fitting, equipment purchases, competition, wagering, handicaps, rulings, or any decision where accuracy matters.

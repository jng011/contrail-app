See how it works

Hit a few balls. Get one clip per ball.

No holding the phone, no trimming video. Set it up once and leave it running.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Stand it behind you.

    Put your iPhone on a stand a few steps back, pointing down your target line, and start a session. Contrail finds the ball on your first swing. If a ball is already down, you can tap it to lock focus.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Record and keep hitting.

    Tap record and hit a few balls, filmed at 240 frames a second. No tapping between swings. Short sessions work best, so stop when you want to see them.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    It finds every swing.

    When you stop, Contrail goes through the video on the phone and cuts each swing into its own clip. Keep it open or let it work in the background.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Your shots, by day.

    Every clip lands in your library, grouped by day, with its tracer already drawn. Favorite the keepers and delete the rest.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Watch it fly. Again.

    Each shot loops with the line drawn over the ball’s flight. Swipe through the session one ball at a time, and favorite or delete a shot right there.

What it changes about range practice.

You stop guessing which shots were good. Every ball gets its own clip and its own line, and you can watch them back whenever you like.

  • See every ball’s flight

    The line shows how each shot started, peaked and curved. You don’t need a launch monitor to see what the ball did.

  • Review a whole session

    Go back through each session one ball at a time, in order, without scrubbing through a long video.

  • Keep the best ones

    Favorite a shot and filter the library down to your favorites. Delete the ones you don’t want.

  • Private by default

    No account, no uploads, no tracking. Your practice stays on your phone.

  • Just your iPhone

    No sensors to clip on and no extra hardware to buy. Something to stand the phone on is all you add.

  • No signal needed

    Recording, finding and tracing all happen on the phone, so a range with no reception is fine.

Questions.

What do I need?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later (it works best on iPhone 15 Pro or later) and something to stand it on behind you, like a small tripod or a phone stand.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Recording, finding and tracing your shots all happen on the iPhone, so it works the same at a range with no signal.

Can I use videos I’ve already filmed?

Not at the moment. Contrail traces sessions you record in the app.

Who do I ask if something isn’t working?

Email sammy@contrailgolf.app or jayden@contrailgolf.app. You can also check the support page.

Where do my shots go?

Into Contrail’s library on your phone, with every session grouped by day. They stay in the app.

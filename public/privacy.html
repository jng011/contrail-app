Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Plain-English summary

Contrail does not collect data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no server.

  • Your range sessions (video, sound, and the phone's motion, location and compass heading) are recorded and processed on your iPhone, and they stay there.
  • We, the people who make Contrail, never receive your videos, your shots, your location or anything else from the app. There is nowhere for the app to send them.
  • Full session recordings are kept out of iCloud Backup. The shots you keep are small and are included in your iPhone's backup like other app data.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Contrail never sees your payment details.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts.
  • Delete shots in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

The rest of this page says the same thing in full detail, permission by permission. If anything here is unclear, email sammy@contrailgolf.app or jayden@contrailgolf.app.

1. Who we are

Contrail is an iPhone app for golfers, made by two independent developers, Sammy and Jayden, based in California, United States. In this policy, "Contrail", "we", "us" and "our" mean the developers of Contrail, and "you" and "your" mean anyone who uses the app or visits this website.

Contrail is currently in pre-release and is coming soon to the App Store. People may also be testing pre-release builds through Apple's TestFlight service. This policy covers every build of the app, released or pre-release, unless a section says otherwise.

What the app does, in short: you put your iPhone on a stand or on the ground a few steps behind you, looking down the target line, and record a short range session. Contrail finds each swing, cuts each one into its own clip and draws the ball's flight over the video. Your shots are kept in a library on your iPhone, grouped by day, where you can favorite, filter and delete them.

You can reach us at sammy@contrailgolf.app or jayden@contrailgolf.app. Section 19 has more on contacting us.

3. Data we do not collect

Most privacy policies start with a long list of what a company collects. Ours starts with what we do not, because that is most of the story. Contrail has no server, so the app has nowhere to send information to us. As a result, we do not collect, receive, store, sell, share, rent or trade any of the following from the app:

  • Identity: no account, sign-in, name, email address, phone number, username or password. Contrail works without you ever telling it who you are.
  • Your recordings: no session videos, shot clips, audio, thumbnails or traced flights. They never leave your device through Contrail.
  • Location: no precise or approximate location, compass heading or list of places you play. Location is used on your iPhone and saved with your sessions there, not sent to us.
  • Sensor data: no motion, tilt, air pressure, depth or Watch movement data.
  • Usage and analytics: no analytics software, no event tracking, no screen views, no session replays, no product telemetry and no crash-reporting software inside the app.
  • Advertising: no ads, no advertising identifiers (Contrail never requests the IDFA) and no ad networks.
  • Tracking: Contrail does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use or link to data brokers.
  • Contacts, photos and files: Contrail never reads your contacts, calendar, messages or existing photo library. If it asks for photo access at all, it asks for "add only" access, which lets it add items but not see what is already there.
  • Payment details: purchases go through Apple; we never see your card or billing information.
  • Health records: Contrail does not read from or write to the Health app.
  • Biometrics: Contrail does not identify people. It does not perform face recognition and does not create face, voice or body prints.

The app's App Store privacy details reflect this. Apple's label for an app that collects nothing is "Data Not Collected". If that ever changes, we will update both this policy and the App Store label before the change ships (see Section 18).

4. Data processed on your device

To do its job, Contrail creates and uses information on your iPhone. "On your device" means the information is created, processed and stored inside the app's private storage on your iPhone, which iOS walls off from other apps, and is not transmitted to us or to anyone else by Contrail.

4.1 Recording a session

When you start recording, Contrail captures video from the camera, sound from the microphone, the phone's motion and orientation, air pressure, the compass heading and, if you allow it, your location. On iPhones that have a LiDAR scanner, it also uses depth information from the camera system. All of this is used to set up the shot and draw the tracer, and it is written to the app's storage on your iPhone.

Video at high frame rates is large. Recording uses a lot of storage and makes the phone warm, which is why Contrail is designed around short sessions: a few swings, stop, and let it process.

4.2 Finding and cutting your shots

After you stop recording, Contrail looks through the session on your iPhone to find each swing, cuts each one into its own short clip and draws the ball's flight over it. This processing happens entirely on your iPhone. No part of it is sent to a server, because there isn't one.

4.3 Your shot library

The shots Contrail cuts are kept in a library in the app, grouped by day. Each kept shot may include its video clip, the traced flight, the date and time, and the sensor information saved with that session (such as location and compass heading). Favorites and filters are stored the same way. You can delete any shot from the library at any time.

4.4 Full session recordings

By default, Contrail keeps only the shots it cuts and discards the full, continuous recording of the session once processing is done. Settings includes a "Keep Full Recordings" option, which is off by default. If you turn it on, Contrail also keeps the whole session (video and sensor data) on your iPhone, which can use a lot of storage. Full recordings are excluded from iCloud Backup (see Section 7). Turning the option off stops new full recordings being kept.

4.5 Settings and preferences

Contrail stores your settings, such as your choices on the Settings screen and whether you have seen the introduction, in the app's own preferences on your iPhone. They are used only to make the app behave the way you set it up.

4.6 Purchase status

Where the app offers purchases, it checks what you have bought by asking Apple's in-app purchase system on your iPhone. That system tells the app what you are entitled to; it does not tell the app, or us, who you are or how you paid. See Section 8.

4.7 Sharing and saving, only when you choose

If you choose to share a recording or save a shot to your photo library, using controls in the app where they are offered, iOS hands the file to the destination you pick (for example, the Photos app, Messages or AirDrop). From that point the copy is governed by the destination's own settings and privacy terms, and by the people you send it to. Contrail does not receive a copy and does not learn where you sent it.

5. Permissions, one by one

iOS asks for your permission before an app can use most sensors. Contrail asks only for what it needs and only when it needs it. For each permission below: what it is for, what happens if you say no, and how to change your mind. Every permission can be turned off at any time in the iOS Settings app; Contrail keeps working as far as it can without it.

5.1 Camera

Why: to film your range session so Contrail can find each shot and draw the tracer. On iPhones with a LiDAR scanner, depth information from the camera system is covered by this same permission.

Where it goes: the video stays on your iPhone.

If you say no: Contrail cannot record, so it cannot make new shots. Your existing library still works.

To change it: Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera › Contrail, or Settings › Apps › Contrail.

5.2 Microphone

Why: the sound of each strike helps Contrail find your shots.

Where it goes: audio stays on your iPhone, as part of your recording.

If you say no: Contrail can still record video, but finding your shots may be less reliable, and your clips will have no sound.

To change it: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › Contrail.

5.3 Motion & Fitness (motion sensors and barometer)

Why: Contrail reads the phone's tilt, steadiness and air pressure (from the barometer) to keep the tracer line accurate. While you set up, it also uses the phone's orientation to show a level.

Where it goes: it stays on your iPhone.

If you say no: Contrail still works, but traces may be less accurate.

To change it: Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness › Contrail.

5.4 Location, while using the app (including compass heading)

Why: Contrail saves your location and compass heading with each session, so the tracer knows which way the ball flies. Contrail asks only for "While Using the App" access. It never asks for "Always" access and does not use your location in the background when you aren't using it.

Where it goes: it is saved with your sessions and shots on your iPhone and is never sent to us. Keep in mind that if you share a recording or a shot yourself, the file you share may carry location information, depending on the destination and your iOS settings.

If you say no: Contrail still records and traces, but cannot use direction and place information, so traces may be less accurate. You can also allow location but turn off Precise Location.

To change it: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Contrail.

5.5 Photos, add only

Why: to add the traced shots you choose to your photo library, where the app offers that option.

What it can't do: "add only" access lets Contrail put new items into your library. It cannot see, read, edit or delete anything already in it.

If you say no: everything else works; Contrail just can't add shots to Photos for you.

To change it: Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Contrail.

5.6 What Contrail never asks for

Contrail does not ask for access to your contacts, calendars, reminders, Bluetooth accessories, local network, Health data, Face ID data, speech recognition, or permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If a future version ever needs a new permission, iOS will ask you first, and we will update this section before that version ships.

6. Apple Watch

Contrail does not currently include an Apple Watch app. We are working on a companion that would use the Watch's own motion sensors to feel the moment you strike the ball, so your iPhone can find each shot more reliably.

If and when a Watch companion ships, it will follow the same rules as the iPhone app:

  • Motion readings from the Watch would be processed on your Watch and your iPhone only.
  • The Watch would talk only to your own paired iPhone, using Apple's built-in watch-to-phone connection, and would not send anything to us or to any server.
  • Any permission the Watch needs (such as Motion & Fitness) would be requested by iOS or watchOS first, and could be turned off in the Settings app or the Watch app on your iPhone.
  • Deleting the Watch app, or Contrail from your iPhone, would remove what it stored.

We will update this section, and the "Last updated" date, before a Watch companion is released.

7. Backups and iCloud

Contrail itself does not use iCloud Drive, iCloud sync or any cloud storage of its own. But iOS may back up your iPhone, including app data, to iCloud or to a computer, if you have turned that on. Here is how that applies to Contrail:

  • Full session recordings are excluded from iCloud Backup. Contrail marks them so iOS leaves them out. They are large, and they stay on the device.
  • Kept shots and settings are stored like normal app data, so they may be included in your iCloud Backup or in a backup to your computer, if you have backups turned on.
  • Those backups are created, stored and protected by Apple (for iCloud) or by your computer (for local backups), under your control and under Apple's terms. We have no access to them.
  • You can manage or turn off iCloud Backup in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › iCloud Backup, and you can choose which apps are included in backups there.

If you restore your iPhone from a backup, Contrail's kept shots come back with it; full session recordings do not.

9. This website and its hosting

Cloudflare may process and log that information as the host, for purposes such as delivering the site, protecting it from attacks and abuse, and running its network, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy). We do not use that information to identify or profile visitors, and we have not added any logging or analytics of our own.

The demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere or saved.

The site may link to other websites, such as Apple's. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Contrail is a general-audience golf app. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the equivalent minimum age where you live). Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect it from children either; recordings a child makes stay on the device they were made on.

The only way we could receive a child's personal information is if a child emailed us. If your child uses Contrail, you can review and delete what it stored on the device at any time, and use iOS Screen Time to manage which apps and permissions your child can use.

13. Retention and deletion

13.1 On your device

Everything Contrail stores stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You control it completely:

  • Delete a shot: open it in the library and delete it.
  • Stop keeping full recordings: make sure "Keep Full Recordings" is off in Settings (it is off by default).
  • Delete everything: delete the Contrail app from your iPhone. iOS removes the app and all of its data, including sessions, shots, settings and location information saved with them.
  • Backups: copies of kept shots in an existing iCloud or computer backup remain until that backup is deleted or replaced. You can delete old backups in your iCloud settings or on your computer.
  • Things you shared: copies you saved to Photos or sent to other people or apps are outside Contrail. Delete them where they now live.

13.2 With us

We hold no data from the app, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete from it. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you and are deleted on request. TestFlight information Apple shares with us is kept only for the duration of testing.

14. Security

The strongest protection is not having the data at all. Because Contrail has no server and sends nothing to us, there is no central database of users' recordings or locations that could be breached.

On your iPhone, Contrail's files live in the app's sandbox, a private area that other apps can't read. iOS encrypts the data on your device when it is protected by a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. We strongly recommend setting a passcode, keeping iOS up to date, and turning on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud if you want your backups end-to-end encrypted.

The security of your device, your backups and the people and apps you choose to share with is outside our control. No software is perfect. If we ever learn of a security issue in Contrail, we will work to fix it promptly and, where the law requires, tell affected users.

This website is served only over HTTPS.

15. International users

We are based in the United States. Contrail works the same way wherever you are: app data stays on your device and is not transferred to us or to any other country by the app. The only cross-border transfers that can involve us are emails you send us, which are handled by our email provider and may be stored in the United States, and TestFlight information that Apple shares with us. When you browse this website, your request is served by our host's network, which operates data centres around the world.

If you write to us from outside the United States, you understand that your message will be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those where you live. Where the law requires a legal basis for transferring your information, we rely on the fact that the transfer is necessary for us to respond to the message you chose to send.

16. Your privacy rights

Privacy laws in many places give you rights over your personal information, such as the right to know what a business holds about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. We support those rights fully. In practice, because we hold no personal information from the app, there is usually nothing on our side to access, correct, delete or opt out of. All of your app data is already in your hands, on your device, and you can view or delete it there at any time (Section 13).

The rights below do apply to emails you send us and to TestFlight information Apple shares with us.

16.1 California residents (CCPA and CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, California residents have the right to know what personal information a business collects, uses and discloses; to access and get a copy of it; to delete it; to correct it; to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing"; to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

  • Categories collected in the last 12 months: from the app, none. From people who email us: identifiers (email address and, if given, name) and the content of their messages. From TestFlight testers, through Apple: identifiers (name and email address if invited by email) and app usage and diagnostic information about pre-release builds.
  • Sources: you, when you email us; Apple, for TestFlight.
  • Purposes: replying to you, providing support, and testing and improving pre-release builds.
  • Disclosure: we do not disclose it to anyone except the service providers that store it for us (such as our email provider).
  • Sale and sharing: we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and have not done so in the last 12 months. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of anyone under 16.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not collect precise location or any other sensitive personal information. The precise location Contrail uses never leaves your device.
  • Shine the Light: we do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

16.2 Other U.S. states

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws, including Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Virginia and others, may have rights to confirm whether we process their personal data, and to access, correct, delete and get a portable copy of it, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not engage in targeted advertising, sale or such profiling. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with the subject "Privacy appeal"; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state's attorney general.

16.3 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR and UK GDPR)

For the app, we are not a controller of your personal data, because the app processes everything on your device and we receive none of it. For emails you send us, we are the controller, and our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you and supporting the app (and, where you ask us to do something, taking steps at your request). For TestFlight information, Apple and we each process it under Apple's terms; our legal basis is our legitimate interest in testing the app.

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

If you live elsewhere, such as Canada, Australia, Brazil, Japan or South Korea, you may have similar rights under local law. Contact us and we will honour them.

Contrail for iPhone

Getting good traces

  1. Put your iPhone on a stand or the ground a few steps behind you, looking down the target line.
  2. Keep the ball and some sky in the frame. Contrail shows a level while you set up.
  3. Start recording. No need to tap anything: Contrail finds your ball on the first swing. If a ball is already down, you can tap it to lock focus and exposure on it.
  4. Hit as usual. Stop when you're done; Contrail finds the shots for you.

Trial and purchase

Start the 7-day free trial whenever you're ready. After that, Contrail is a one-time $2.99 unlock; nothing renews and nothing is charged automatically. Bought it before? Tap Restore purchases.

iOS 26 or later. Works best on iPhone 15 Pro or later.

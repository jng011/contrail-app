Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Contrail does not collect data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, and no server.

Data on your iPhone

Contrail records your range sessions (video, sound, and the phone's motion, location and compass heading) and keeps them in the app on your iPhone to find and trace your shots. Full session recordings are kept out of iCloud Backup; the shots you keep are small and are included in your iPhone's backup like other app data. Contrail shares nothing with anyone. Delete shots in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Permissions

  • Camera: to film your session.
  • Microphone: the sound of each strike helps find your shots.
  • Motion & Fitness: phone tilt and air pressure keep the tracer accurate.
  • Location (while using the app): your location and compass heading tell Contrail which way the ball flies.
  • Photos (add only): to save the traced shots you choose.

You can turn any of these off in iOS Settings.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. Contrail never sees your payment details.
